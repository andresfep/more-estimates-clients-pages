Common Questions

How much does a custom closet cost?

It depends on the size of your space, the materials, and the features you choose — drawers, islands, lighting, and so on. A reach-in closet costs far less than a full walk-in. Your designer confirms an exact, all-in price during your free in-home consult, and with this event you'll get 50% off installation on top of it.

Am I signing up for a pitch?

No. The consult is a working design session: your designer measures, listens to how you use the space, and shows you a 3D layout with pricing. There's no obligation and no pressure to decide on the spot.

Can I start with just one closet?

Absolutely. Many homeowners start with a single closet or pantry and add more later. The 50% off installation offer applies to any qualifying project, large or small.

How long does installation take?

Most single closets are installed in one day. Larger walk-ins or multiple rooms may take two. Your designer gives you a firm schedule before anything is ordered.

Is financing really 0%?

Yes — qualified homeowners can choose 0% APR promotional financing with $0 down options and low monthly payments. Financing is subject to credit approval; your designer will walk you through the plans available.
