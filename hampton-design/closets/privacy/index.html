Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what Hampton Design & Closets ("Hampton Design", "we", "us") collects on this website, hamptondesign.moreestimatesco.site, how we use it and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you give us: the answers to the quote questions (the space you want to organize and your ZIP code), your name, mobile number and email, and the appointment you book.
  • What your browser sends: your device and browser type, IP address, the pages you view on this site, and the ad or link that brought you here, including its campaign tags and click identifiers.
  • Cookies and the Meta Pixel: this site uses the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies and reports visits, quote requests and bookings to Meta.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your project by phone call, text message and email.
  • To schedule, confirm and remind you of your in-home design consultation.
  • To prepare your design and your quote.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and this website.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run our scheduling, messaging, customer records, website and marketing for us. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • Meta, through the Meta Pixel. When you request a quote, your email, phone number, name and ZIP code are scrambled (hashed) in your browser and sent to Meta so that our ad results can be measured.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your mobile number or your consent to receive text messages with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Calls and text messages

When you submit the quote form, you agree that Hampton Design may call or text you at the number you provided about your project, including with automated technology. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. You can also call (602) 423-5505 to opt out.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling (602) 423-5505.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can manage the ads Meta shows you in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences.
  • You can unsubscribe from our emails with the link in any email.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting duties, then we delete it.

Children

This website is for homeowners aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
